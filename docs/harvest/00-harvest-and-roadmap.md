## 1. TL;DR

1. **This is a re-merge, not a port.** `packages/typescript-config/*`, `drizzle.config.ts`, `packages/ui/src/lib/utils.ts` and `.prettierrc.json` are **byte-identical** across the two repos; `eslint-config` differs by one line; every shared dep resolves to the same major. The donor's own `.env.example:7-9` says its convention was "mirrored from the Camp 404 convention" — **Camp 404 is the older project.** Mechanical porting risk is near zero.

2. **The single biggest win is not a port at all.** `team_memberships` has **zero production INSERTs** — the only one anywhere is `packages/db/src/__tests__/_factories.ts:39` (verified this session). Every `team` / `team_leads` broadcast and questionnaire send resolves to zero recipients **and toasts success**. Eight of 24 units hit this independently. It is WP6 (#130), and it gates roughly eight other items.

3. **Three live bugs the comparison exposed are worth more than any feature.** (a) The questionnaire `image` kind renders `<AvatarUpload>` with no `uploadUrl` → defaults to `/api/uploads/avatar` → writes `avatars/<uid>/` → calls `deleteAvatarBlobs` → **a questionnaire photo deletes the member's profile photo, and vice versa** (verified end to end). (b) `requireCaptain` in `camp-management/actions.ts:92-110` omits the `isApproved` check its twin in `camp-settings/actions.ts:44-63` performs — a pending-approval captain can approve applicants and **decrypt SA IDs**. (c) `decryptOrNull` (`packages/db/src/crypto.ts:72-79`) swallows every decrypt failure and returns `null`, so on a key-rotated or misconfigured deployment **an unreadable encrypted ID is indistinguishable from an absent one** — the captain modal shows a blank ID, the member's replay form pre-fills empty, and nothing anywhere raises. All three are effort S. **[VERIFIED, WITH ONE CORRECTION — see the verification note above: (c) was originally written as silent data destruction; it is not. Both writers guard with `if (idNumber)` (`apps/web/lib/forms.ts:86`, `apps/web/app/onboarding/questionnaire/actions.ts:65`) and the MCP writer treats `undefined` as unchanged (`mcp/tools/profile.ts:447-456`), so no re-save nulls the ciphertext. The silent-absence failure is real; the data loss is not.]**

4. **Six S-sized foundations unblock about half the corpus** (~1.5 days total): the 9-export skeleton kit, `table.tsx`, `decryptField` + `crypto-guard`, `writeAuditEvent` + the `audit_log.metadata` `.$type<>()` fix + 2 indexes, `withTransaction` (15 hand-rolled sites), and a one-line `server-only` vitest alias (named as a blocker by 10 units).

5. **Builder Phase E is the largest genuine feature gap and it comes with a working reference implementation.** Camp 404 has literally zero results code — no aggregation, no CSV, no per-respondent view, no completion tally. The donor ships all four plus a 341-line test companion. `aggregateResponses` is **M, not L** (corrected: `toggle`/`combobox` reuse the `choice` arm unchanged, so it's 2 new arms, not 4).

6. **Camp 404 is richer than the donor in 6 of 9 shared table pairs** — `invite_codes` (max_uses/revoked_at/assigned_rank vs a single-use token), `notification_deliveries` (channel/presentation/push_status — the donor has no delivery model at all), `broadcasts` (scheduling/scope/kind). Camp 404 has **33 tables the donor has never heard of.** The donor is not a superset; it is a differently-shaped product.

7. **Two places the donor genuinely wins on schema:** `audit_events` indexes actor+action+**subject**+**created_at desc** where Camp 404's `audit_log` indexes only actor+action — "everything that happened to member X" and "recent activity" are both seq scans (two `CREATE INDEX`, trivial). And `burner_bios` uses 26 typed columns incl. `privacy_flags` where Camp 404 buries the bio in one JSONB blob — **you cannot index, audit or selectively redact a JSONB field.** That's a structural warning before WP5 (#129) surfaces emergency contacts, not a port.

8. **~40% of the "effort: S" rows are S for the file and M/L for the seam.** 292 of 478 rows are rated S. Named exceptions with their real blockers are in §3 and §7 — the worst offenders are commitlint (18 of the last 40 subjects exceed 72 chars, and `design(...)` is used as a *type* that `config-conventional` rejects), the `loading.tsx` rollout (spec-blocked, see below), and the coverage ratchet (no provider installed in any of 8 workspaces).

9. **The `loading.tsx` rollout is spec-blocked and twelve units missed it.** Six Camp 404 impl specs say *do not create one* (`16-captain-tools.md:43`, `11-invite-tool.md:57`, `09-notifications.md:153`, `05-approval-gate.md:54`, `01-landing.md:27`, `17-mcp-connect.md:125`). `design/spec/flows.md:440` is the narrow defensible line: skeletons are for **client** fetches. **The kit is unconditional; the rollout needs your ruling.** (Verified: 0 `loading.tsx`, 2 error boundaries, 24 `force-dynamic` pages.)

10. **`AGENTS.md:180-182` says the Playwright suite is disabled; `ci.yml:179` runs it on every `src` PR, inside the `ci-pass` aggregate.** Units 01, 02, 06, 09 and 23 each *downgraded* e2e recommendations on that false premise — **re-read those at full value.** One of the two documents is wrong; flag it `UNRESOLVED` rather than guessing.

11. **Camp 404 has zero working outbound channels today.** No email provider anywhere (one false-positive grep). FCM push is inert until Firebase env is set in Vercel (`DEFERRED.md:80-85`). Telegram is deliberately not activated (`DEFERRED.md:65`). Every "send the applicant a rejection reason" and "notify on questionnaire release" item lands in the in-app inbox only.

12. **Half of what's left has no donor reference at all.** 19 of ~35 open roadmap lines returned **zero** donor source hits: reduced-motion, @dnd-kit DragOverlay, the whole FCM pipeline, Telegram, the entire MCP surface, Capacitor, recipes, manuals, the tasks board, adoptees, family tree, carpool, reimbursements, `opt_in` scope, configurable-teams Phase 4, workshops, inventory, dietary. **Anyone reading the ledger as a roadmap will systematically under-weight that half.** §6 is the corrective.

13. **Three high-value donor files were named by zero harvest docs.** `apps/org/components/decision-panel.tsx` (192 L — state-machine-gated approve/reject/request-changes with a mandatory typed reason and a `refusal` prop rendering the server's exact refusal sentence next to the disabled control) answers WP1, WP2 **and** WP9. `apps/web/components/registration/registration-summary.tsx` (467 L) is WP4's S27 screen *and* WP10's read-back surface *and* half of Phase E. `apps/org/components/section-review-thread.tsx` (197 L) is a two-sided reviewer comment thread. Read them before starting those work packages.

14. **The unasked product question: what happens in year two?** The donor scopes ~11 tables by `edition_id`. Camp 404 has no year/edition/cycle concept anywhere (`rg '\bedition\b'` over `packages/db` and `apps/web/lib` returns nothing). On a second burn, `users.dues_paid`, `approval_status`, `terms_consented_at`, every `required_actions` row and every `questionnaire_responses` row carry over with no reset. Not "port the editions table" — **decide it before the dues ledger gets built**, because a dues ledger with no cycle key is the expensive version of this mistake.

15. **Honest total size of what's left.** 83 canonical donor-informed items ≈ 12–16 developer-days if you take the high-value subset and skip the elective tail. The Camp 404 roadmap that has *no* donor reference (WP7/8 a11y, MCP captain tier, Telegram, mobile, recipes/manuals/tasks/dues) is comfortably larger — call it 25–40 days. **The harvest accelerates the smaller half.**

## 4. What Camp 404 already has that is as good or better

## 5. What to deliberately NOT take

**Multi-tenancy and the org/participant split — the entire dimension.** `groups`, `memberships`, all `org_*` tables, `project_roles`, `member_role_assignments`, `wrangler_assignments`, `orgCan`/`orgCanIn`/`orgCanInDomain` over an 8-domain vocabulary, `ENGINEER_RANK_CARVE_OUTS`, `questionnaire-authz.ts`, `AudienceSpec`, `link_app`/`resolveNotificationLinkApp`, `ConsoleGate`, `isParticipantFacingActivation`, `audience-options.ts` prefix encoding, and both `apps/org` (52 files) and `apps/suppliers` (59 files) whole. The donor's own audit calls those two apps "close to being the same application twice" (~2,000 duplicated lines).

> **The rule that prevents removing an authz check by accident:** a donor parameter *named* for a tenant is deletable; a donor parameter that **gates a SELECT list** is not — it becomes a Camp 404 rank parameter. When in doubt, keep the parameter and pass a constant. The dangerous scopes are audience resolution (the donor calls it *a privacy boundary, not routing*), `canReadActivationResults`, and `canReadPersonalInformationIn` **before the select**. `packages/ui` is verified tenant-free (zero hits for `groupId|orgId|tenant|supplierId` across all of its src), so the whole UI layer strips safely.

**Do not port `/directory`.** The donor's directory lists **camps, not people**, deliberately, because a browsable member list is the bulk-exposure surface. Camp 404 already crossed that line at `/captains/camp-management`. The donor's reasoning is an argument against widening what's there, not for importing anything.

**The bespoke auth stack — out of scope by instruction, and unbuildable anyway.** `packages/auth` pins `better-auth` at exactly `1.6.25`; Camp 404's root `package.json:26` sets `pnpm.overrides["better-auth"] = "~1.4.18"` under `@neondatabase/auth 0.4.1-beta`, so **any donor file importing better-auth resolves to a lower minor** — and the donor's own AGENTS.md forbids moving the pin, citing two high-severity advisories. All 2FA (618 + 137 L) and passkeys (275 L) are **provider-blocked, not merely unbuilt**. The email-change 48h state machine is moot: Camp 404's `users` table has no email column at all.

**AfrikaBurn domain.** The `years` question kind (hardcodes 2007–2026 and `NO_BURN_YEARS [2020,2021]`), `sound.ts`, `placement-zones.ts`, camp categories, supplier standing, the 42-column `registrations` table with its 32 `s1_`–`s6_` fields, `QuiltBand` (fills from `--color-ab-*` tokens that don't exist here, so it renders **invisible diamonds with no error**), `DOMAIN_PROMPT`, the 90-entry reserved-username list, and `docs/sources/**` (43 MB, 816 scraped pages).

**Over-engineering for 30–80 people.** Chunked notification inserts (Camp 404 binds ~640 params against Postgres' 65,535 ceiling — it cannot occur without the camp growing 100×, and the donor's own two comments contradict each other on the figure); deterministic seeded shuffle (FNV-1a + xorshift32 — position bias is not measurable at this size, and the donor's own note admits everyone gets the same order anyway); the advisory-lock migration runner (280 lines to serialise three concurrent Vercel builders; Camp 404 has one — take `planMigration()`/`isPoolerConnection()` alone); the 8-shard persona e2e matrix and the 8-shard coverage matrix; `.github/CODEOWNERS` on a single-maintainer repo; deep permission delegation; enumeration detection and volume thresholds (the donor **built one and deliberately removed it**, and `AGENTS.md:176-180` forbids it here — reading many members' records in one sitting *is* the work).

**New dependencies that reverse written decisions.** `PhoneInput` (reverses `packages/types/src/questionnaire.ts:7-9`, "lenient formats — no new deps"; costs `react-phone-number-input` + `libphonenumber-js`); the Tiptap `MarkdownEditor` (5 deps, a jsdom shim, a shipped `aria-pressed` staleness bug, and `09-notifications.md:173` is a *decision*: "body is plain text"); Radix Tabs (`SegmentedControl` already covers it); ToggleGroup; Radix Accordion. **Side finding worth acting on: `apps/web/package.json:44-45` declares `react-markdown ^10.1.0` and `rehype-sanitize ^6.0.0` and nothing imports either — delete them.**

The client-error ring buffer (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-31-shake-to-report-design.md:77` has a "Deliberately out of scope" bullet attributed to you).
