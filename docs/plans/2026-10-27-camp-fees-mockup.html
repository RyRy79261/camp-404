3 · "Coming this year?" → "What can you commit?"

Shown only after "Coming". The Join site's floating scale: slide or type ANY amount in rands (more than Perfect World is fine). The scale's names are marks under the slider; the dollar label sits beside the rand figure. The marked point is the Recommended amount, about US$450. Then "By when can you pay it?", and a subsidy tick box: when ticked, R0 is fine and "By when" is optional. Nothing is charged until captains give the member a place.
