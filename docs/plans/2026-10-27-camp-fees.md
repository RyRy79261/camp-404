# Camp fees sprint (2026-10-27)

Owner-approved after the camp leads meeting of 2026-10-27 (tent deposit
R70,000 due 1 Dec, storage R4,400 a month, go/no-go about 21 Nov; the only
priority until mid-November is sign-ups and fee commitments). The approved
mock-up is `2026-10-27-camp-fees-mockup.html` (+ `.png`, `-phone.png`): match
it, do not re-derive it. Where it shows options, the "Picked" one is the
design. Sample amounts and names in it are samples.

One PR, branch `feat/camp-fees`. Parts:

## F. Foundation (schema, core, db)

- Pledge = what a member commits and by when. **No fixed fee amounts**
  (owner): the amounts are the Join site's floating scale (`fee` in the camp's
  join-site config, `packages/types/src/join-site.ts`, drawn by
  `apps/join/components/os/windows/fee-scale.tsx`): Essential, Reasonable,
  Ideal, Perfect World, the dollar rate a captain typed, the Subsidy note.
  The Recommended mark is about US$450 at that rate. A member may commit ANY
  whole-rand amount (above Perfect World too). `fee_tiers` must not become a
  second source of amounts; the pledge stores the member's own amount.
- "By when" is one of six fixed values (plain words in the mock-up): all by
  end of Nov, all by end of Dec, part by end of Nov and the rest later, part
  by end of Dec and the rest later, all by 15 Jan 2027, I'll talk to a
  captain. Store as a fixed enum (year-relative wording in core, never a date
  typed by the member).
- "I need a subsidy: please talk to me" is a flag on the pledge. With it, R0
  is allowed and "by when" is optional. Flagged members get no fee reminders.
- Andrew's published builder questionnaire `camp-fees-the-money-oracle` asks
  the same "when" question (options `option_1`..`option_6` in that order).
  Add a question role (the `participation_intent` pattern, see
  `QUESTION_ROLES` / builder roles) for "fee timing" with fixed option
  values; a custom data migration gives his question the role and maps the
  stored answers, so his answers and the pledge's "by when" are one field.
- Next instalment: derived from the pledge amount and "by when" (part = half
  now, the rest at the next step, e.g. R7,200 "part by end of Nov" =
  R3,600 by 30 Nov then R3,600 by 31 Dec), unless Finance set a payment plan
  (`dues_instalments`), which wins. Pure, in `packages/core`.
- Dues year gets a "Collecting fees" switch (compare-and-set, audited).
- Camp bank details for members to pay into: South Africa (bank, account
  name, number, branch code) and abroad (SWIFT, account number, bank address,
  a note on transfer fees). Finance edits them in Finance settings, audited.
  The owner types the real values; nothing seeded.
- Camp money: `camp_balances` (amount, as-of date, who typed it; history
  kept) and `camp_bills` (name, payee, amount, due date, one time or monthly,
  planned / not paid / paid, paid on). Who: `MONEY_KEEPER`. Every write a
  compare-and-set with its audit row (new audit actions in core).
- Forecast (pure, core): balance + fees still expected (pledges − paid, not
  counting subsidy flags) − bills still due − team budgets not spent = money
  left, and the date-by-date list with "money after" (fee "in" lines from
  the derived instalments; monthly bills expanded per month).
- Each new table gets its `TABLE_LIFECYCLE` entry (year, erasure).

## M. Finance screens (Payments program)

- New tab **Camp money**: mock-up step 1, Option B (four figures, then date
  by date with Still due / Paid, "Mark paid" as the one primary row action,
  "+ Add a bill", the bank balance with Update and its history).
- **Who owes what**: step 2. Pledged (own amount + nearest scale name),
  When they can pay, Paid, Still owing, Next instalment; totals; filters
  Coming / Maybe / Owe money / Subsidy / Everyone; "Collecting fees" switch;
  "Ask everyone to pledge" (outline) and "Remind who still owes" (primary)
  with the step 2 dialog.
- Finance settings: camp bank details.

## P. Member side

- "Coming this year?" → after Coming: "What can you commit?" (the Join
  site's slider, any amount, Recommended mark), "By when can you pay it?",
  the subsidy tick (step 3). This is the pledge write.
- Pay guide card, step 4 Option A: a card rising from the tray bottom right
  (phone: banner above the bottom bar, opens as a sheet), only while
  collecting is on and the member owes and is not subsidy-flagged. It talks
  about the NEXT instalment. Reference with Copy, From South Africa / From
  abroad, Send your proof of payment, "Remind me tomorrow" (hides for a day,
  in the browser only). Never over the blocking layer or held desktop.
- My dues: "Next payment" at the top (step 5).
- Reminders: "Ask everyone to pledge" and "Remind who still owes" use the
  nudge shape (`packages/db/src/nudges.ts`): a non-blocking required action
  and one notice each, never stacking. Each notice is about the member's OWN
  next payment (inbox, push, email, Ol' Jeb DM). The Telegram group gets only
  a general post that names nobody and shows no amounts (owner 2A). An
  automatic reminder when an instalment is due, in `runDueWork` (camp
  daytime, deduped).

## A. Audience and account

- **Not coming = no group sends** (owner): `computeAudience` leaves out a
  member whose participation this year is `not_attending`, for every group
  scope (everyone, team, team_leads, drivers, opt-in), captains included.
  `individual` still reaches them; so do notices about their own account.
  Maybe and not-answered still get them. The send screens and the composer
  say "Reaches N people. Left out: M who said Not coming this year. Who?"
  (step 8). Questionnaire reminders, Telegram tags and team-leads calendar
  invites follow the same rule. Saying Not coming also stops their open
  questionnaire required actions blocking and reminding them (owner A); they
  may still answer. Fix the AGENTS.md line "gates nothing yet ... every
  audience" with a `[CORRECTION 2026-10-27]`.
- **Send password reset link** on the captain's member panel (step 7):
  captains only, confirm, audited, the same Better Auth reset email.
- **Profile checklist** (step 6): one grouped bell notice "Your profile isn't
  finished: N things missing" and the same card on Profile: Connect Telegram
  (the DM link), Telegram username, display name still an email address,
  emergency contact. Each line links to its fix and ticks itself. Never a
  block, computed on read (no stored notice rows per gap).
